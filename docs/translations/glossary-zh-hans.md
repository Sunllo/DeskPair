## Register and tone

- Reader is addressed as 你 throughout, never 您; sentences stay short and declarative like the English, with no marketing intensifiers added.
- Vocabulary is localized to mainland/Singapore usage rather than converted character by character (服务器, 网络, 文件, 进程, 哈希, 令牌, 码率, 局域网, 互联网). "电脑" is kept over "计算机" because it reads naturally in both mainland China and Singapore/Malaysia.
- OS UI labels follow the Simplified Chinese builds: Windows “更多信息” / “仍要运行”, 未知发布者; macOS “打开”, “系统设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 屏幕录制”.
- Quotation marks are “ ” (not 「」); dashes are —— without surrounding spaces; spaces between Chinese and Latin text follow the Traditional reference.
- Legal pages use slightly more formal wording (本条款, 按现状提供, 适销性, 后果性损失, 以英文文本为准) but are translated sentence by sentence with no caveat softened or dropped.
- "beta" follows the reference: 测试期间 on the home page, "beta" kept on the pricing and terms pages.
