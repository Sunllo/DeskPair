## Register and tone

- Body text is 합쇼체 (-습니다/-합니다); instructions use -하십시오. No exclamation marks, no superlatives.
- FAQ questions use the -나요 ending, as Korean help centres do; their answers return to -습니다.
- Buttons, navigation, list items and headings use short noun forms (로그인, 계속, 디스플레이 간 전환).
- "You" is left out wherever Korean allows it; where a subject is needed it is 사용자 (never 당신/귀하).
- "We/our" is 저희 on the product pages and 당사 on the legal pages (privacy.*, terms.*).
- "Link" (a device to an account) is 연동, kept apart from 연결, which is reserved for a remote connection. Particles after placeholders are avoided by restructuring ("{0} 앞으로", "{0}에서", "{0} 하나뿐이며").
