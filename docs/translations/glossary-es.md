## Register and tone

- One form of address site-wide: **tú** (no vosotros, no voseo, no usted), including privacy and terms.
- Plain and dry like the English: no superlatives, no exclamation marks; caveats and warnings kept at full strength.
- "Computadora" throughout; "teléfono" (never "celular" or "móvil"); "ratón" for mouse; "archivo" for file.
- "Cifrado/cifrar", never "encriptado". "Clave" is reserved for cryptographic keys, "contraseña" for passwords.
- OS dialog labels follow the Spanish UI of each system: Más información / Ejecutar de todas formas (Windows); Abrir, Ajustes del Sistema, Privacidad y seguridad, Grabación de pantalla (macOS).
- Parenthetical dashes use the Spanish raya attached to the clause (—así—); dates as "5 de marzo de 2026", months lower case.
