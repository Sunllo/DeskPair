## Register and tone

- Address: «вы» with a lower-case letter; imperative in the polite plural (откройте, введите).
- No ё anywhere (удаленный, учетная, еще); sentences are built so that «все/всё» is never ambiguous.
- Sober, declarative, no exclamation marks and no superlatives; caveats and limits are kept at full strength.
- Placeholders stand where no case ending is needed: after «по адресу», after a colon, or inside «…» as a page name
  («на странице «{0}»»). `auth.signin.title` is the noun «Вход» because it is inserted into such sentences.
- Legal pages follow the usual Russian formulas («как есть», «явных или подразумеваемых», «упущенная выгода»).
- Guillemet quotes «…», em dash with spaces, thousands grouped with a plain space (600 000).
