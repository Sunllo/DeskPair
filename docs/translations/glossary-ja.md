## Register and tone

- です・ます体 throughout; plain and direct, no honorific stacking (no 〜させていただきます), no exclamation marks, no superlatives.
- "You" is mostly dropped as is natural in Japanese; あなた appears only where the legal text needs an explicit subject.
- "We" is 私たち on marketing/help pages and 当社 on privacy.* and terms.* (standard for Japanese legal text).
- サインイン is used for both the OS sign-in and the website account, matching Microsoft's Japanese UI; ログイン is not used.
- Long vowels follow the current Microsoft style (コンピューター, サーバー, ブラウザー, フォルダー). Full-width 、。「」, half-width alphanumerics and parentheses, no space between Japanese and Latin text.
- OS UI labels are quoted as they appear in Japanese systems: 「詳細情報」→「実行」(SmartScreen), 「開く」,「システム設定」>「プライバシーとセキュリティ」>「画面収録」(macOS).
